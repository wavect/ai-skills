# Distinguishing a mismatch from missing evidence

A reference customer from another industry can still be relevant if the workflow, stakes and constraints match. Explain which dimension does or does not transfer. A short page can be sufficient when a warm referral supplies context. Verify the entry channel before recommending another proof block.

For a suspected device issue, name the viewport observed and the action affected. Text alone cannot establish a hidden CTA or a broken form. For a paid campaign, compare the ad promise with the destination. A traffic-mix change can alter overall conversion while each segment's rate stays stable.
