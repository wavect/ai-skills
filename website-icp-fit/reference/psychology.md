# Comprehension and buyer behavior

Treat an awareness level or the Fogg model as a way to form a question. Does the buyer understand the outcome, have the information needed to act and see the next step? If a visitor cannot locate implementation requirements, inspect link text and navigation before adding urgency.

Give a matching participant a realistic goal without naming the desired button. Observe their first action, completion and explanation. Keep participant observations separate from an expert prediction. A short hero exposure can test recall of audience and offer. A task tracing the form can test ability to proceed.
