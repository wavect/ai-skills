# Tracing the decision path

Walk from entry page to the intended commitment. At each step record the buyer's open question, what the page answers and what information the CTA requests. A demo form asking for budget may qualify sales calls or block buyers who need scope before asking for funds. Verify which role is using it.

Place proof near the claim it supports. A customer quote about ease of setup supplies different evidence from an independent certification. For pricing, identify unit, interval, exclusions and how a buyer can estimate their bill. Suggest a specific edit and measure qualified completions through the affected step.

## Example funnel read

Suppose qualified ad traffic produces 120 mobile landings, 30 CTA taps, 12 form starts and three completions. Report both 3/120 overall and 3/12 after form start. The figures locate a step worth observing. They do not establish whether the cause is form length, a browser error, lower intent or a tracking problem. Compare like-for-like traffic and verify the completion event before changing copy. For a test, keep the audience and destination stable and change the line associated with the observed hesitation.
