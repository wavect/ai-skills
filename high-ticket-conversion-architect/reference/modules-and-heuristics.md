# Qualification, ROI and proof options

For each application field, ask who uses the answer and what changes. A rollout deadline can route an urgent project to an available team. Drop a revenue question if the team never uses it. Review completion, qualified-call rate and wins with the same eligibility rule and cohort window.

When the buyer requests an ROI model, separate their inputs, observed delivery costs and assumptions. Provide a break-even calculation with units. For a €20,000 engagement and an assumed €2,000 monthly net benefit, simple payback is ten months after benefits begin. Label the figure illustrative until comparable results and timing support it.

Use actual scope boundaries, published terms and documented capacity. A redacted sample can demonstrate the method if the owner permits sharing. A client outcome needs substantiation and permission to publish. In an audit, return one supported change and the sales data that would test it.
