# High-Ticket Conversion Architect

Collect the offer, buyer and decision makers, typical source of traffic, price or pricing terms, next step, constraints and usable proof. For a rewrite, start from the supplied page and requested block. For a new page, draft from known facts and keep editorial questions visibly separate from publishable copy.

## Draft the buying decision

| Block | What the buyer needs |
| --- | --- |
| Headline | Name the buyer's job and the concrete deliverable. Use an outcome claim only if the brief supports it. |
| Context | Describe the trigger, the current alternative and the consequence in the buyer's own terms. |
| Mechanism | Show what happens during delivery, who does the work and what the buyer receives. Name an actual artifact. |
| Proof | Pair the claim with a permitted case, sample or inspectable process. State what that evidence establishes. |
| Fit | State the prerequisites and a meaningful reason another option might suit the buyer. |
| Next step | Say what the buyer submits, what happens after submission and what commitment it entails. |
| Terms | Surface scope limits, price basis, responsibilities and relevant guarantees only as supplied. |

Use these blocks as a wireframe. Prioritize the requested section on a partial brief. A warm referral may need scope and price immediately. Cold traffic may first need a way to inspect the work. Keep qualification questions only when their answers change eligibility, routing or preparation.

## Deliver copy a team can use

For each requested block, return copy, placement and its proof source or an editorial dependency. When results are confidential, use a redacted artifact only with permission, or describe a verified deliverable. Keep unapproved claims, customer names, quantified ROI, deadlines and guarantees out of the publishable draft.

For a full page, end with the primary CTA, the qualification rule and a measurement plan that follows eligible visitors through qualified calls, proposals and wins. Use the client's sales cycle to choose the review window. A rise in bookings can coexist with a fall in qualified pipeline.

**Worked example.** For a €20k assessment with a confirmed handoff checklist and no public case study, offer to show the checklist if it can be shared. Base proof on its contents. Leave “average payback” in the evidence request until a comparable customer outcome is documented.
