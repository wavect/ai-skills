# Behavioral checks

Use a fresh session for each case. Supply the prompt and selected skill, then review the output separately. Score the decision and its supporting evidence. Wording may vary.

| Skill | Prompt | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| pmf-advisor | We surveyed 80 active accounts. Of 20 respondents, 14 would be very disappointed. Nine of 25 eligible accounts filed again. Should we scale ads? | Separates 70% among respondents, 25% response rate and 36% repeat filing among eligible accounts. Proposes a decision tied to acquisition economics and a mature cohort. |
| icp-discovery | One clinic bought when it opened a second site. Another renewed its old tool. Who should we interview, and what would distinguish the two? | Compares the two clinics, treats the second-site trigger as provisional and asks about the last real decision. |
| pricing-strategy | At €100 a month, 20 of 200 prospects signed. At €150, 15 of 200 signed, all inbound. Cost is €55 per paying account. Should we raise prices? | Shows €4.50 and about €7.13 first-month contribution per eligible prospect. Identifies channel selection and proposes a comparable test before changing existing accounts. |
| qa-advisor | Our retry test expects HTTP 200 and uses a stub store. What can we tell the release team about duplicate reservations? | Classifies duplicate writes as unverified. Asserts one persisted reservation for the same key at a boundary that can observe the store. |
| website-icp-fit | Compliance leads arrive from a checklist ad. Our hero says “Operations for everyone. Book a call.” Which line should we change first? | Quotes the mismatch, makes copy conditional on actual checklist availability and identifies a measurable next action. Text alone cannot explain a mobile drop. |
| high-ticket-conversion-architect | Our €25k migration assessment delivers a worksheet and risk register. Our client case is confidential. Draft just the proof block. | Returns only usable proof copy and relevant editorial dependencies. Grounds the claim in confirmed deliverables and preserves confidentiality. |
