# Buying roles and a reachability test

For a recent deal, record who experienced the problem, who evaluated the solution, who released funds and who could veto it. Attach a decision or artifact to each role. In a solo practice one person can occupy all four roles.

Turn the segment into an observable search criterion. A newly advertised second shift can be visible before outreach. “Frustrated by scheduling” generally requires an interview. For a bounded channel test record time spent, organizations screened, matches, reachable roles and unknown triggers. Counting 50 names in two hours is one possible trial. Set the target to fit channel economics and account value.

Report where access is unverified. Define a short recruitment message around the last relevant event and run outreach only when the user requested it.

## Example recruitment brief

Recruit the person who chose the scheduling tool when a practice expanded to its second location. Include one practice that switched and one that retained its incumbent. Ask each to bring the last quote, renewal notice or approval email they are willing to discuss. “Who first noticed the issue, and who could have stopped the purchase?” distinguishes the person who feels the pain from the budget holder.

Record the event, role, current tool and source for each candidate. Mark the final decision and purchase authority unknown until confirmed. A channel test can end with “12 practices screened, four openings verified, one decision maker reachable” without extrapolating a market size.
