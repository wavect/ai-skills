# Switch interviews and segment comparison

Use a real change of behavior as the anchor. Ask “What was happening the first time you looked for another option?” Then reconstruct the previous workaround, first search, alternatives, purchase and first use. Record the push away from the old workflow, pull of the new one, anxiety about change and habit that favored staying. Preserve direct quotes separately from your interpretation.

Compare wins with no-decisions on the trigger that should separate them. If the same trigger appears in both, inspect urgency, authority and onboarding capacity. If several churned accounts never completed setup, investigate onboarding before naming an anti-ICP. Keep counts, contrary cases and sampling source with the segment definition.
