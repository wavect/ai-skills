# Margins, expansion and price changes

For a recurring account compute revenue and variable cost over the same interval. If usage triples after a customer upgrades, include infrastructure and incremental service labor in the heavy-use example. Show the shortfall if the current plan loses money at that usage level.

For an existing revenue cohort, net revenue retention = (opening recurring revenue + expansion − contraction − churn) / opening recurring revenue. Exclude revenue from newly acquired accounts. Specify interval and currency. A short cohort history makes a lifetime-value estimate highly sensitive to assumed churn. Show payback or scenario ranges until enough renewals mature.

For a price increase, identify account groups by contract and renewal date, calculate the bill under each offer using their observed usage, and model contraction or churn. Draft a clear change notice with the effective date, options and contact path. Discounts should have a defined reason, duration and renewal treatment.
