# Pricing Strategy

Clarify the offer, customer segment, currency, billing interval and the pricing decision. Keep list price, quoted price, realized price and stated willingness to pay distinct. Reuse supplied deal history before asking for more data.

## Build a Pricing Strategy Card

1. **Value and cost.** Describe the buyer's current alternative and the unit of value they recognize. Show assumptions behind any savings estimate. Identify variable delivery, support and payment costs. Distinguish a sustainable cost floor from a buyer's willingness to pay.
2. **Metric and package.** Compare a plausible alternative with the proposed unit (per account, seat, completed job or usage). Show what happens to a light and a heavy customer. Look for units that punish inviting teammates, surprise the budget holder or let costs grow without revenue.
3. **Price evidence.** Use comparable paid deals and losses where possible. Keep segments and terms separate. With survey data, report method, valid sample and limitations. With no willingness-to-pay evidence, give a testable offer and route to evidence. Identify illustrative amounts as such.
4. **Decision.** State the unit, included usage, overage or upgrade trigger, interval and trade-off. For existing customers consider contract terms and renewal timing. Specify what you would quote to the next eligible prospect, what to measure and what would reverse the decision.

## Compare experiments fairly

Use contemporaneous comparable prospects or consistently assigned accounts where feasible. Track contribution per eligible account over the same observation window, alongside conversion, churn and support burden. Record discounts, mix shifts and immature deals. Base sample planning on baseline, minimum meaningful effect and traffic. Small pipelines can support a quote-and-objection log while evidence accumulates.

**Worked example.** At 10 paid accounts per 100 eligible prospects, a €100 monthly package with €60 variable cost contributes €400 for the first month. At 8 per 100, a €140 package with the same €60 cost contributes €640. Compare €4.00 and €6.40 per prospect, then check assignment, uncertainty and later retention before rollout.
