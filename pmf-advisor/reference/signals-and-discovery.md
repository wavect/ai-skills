# Discovery, product cadence and pivot decisions

Ask a buyer to walk through the last occurrence of the problem. What triggered the task? What did they actually do? Who contributed time or money? Ask to see an anonymized artifact when that would resolve uncertainty.

Match the signal to the job. For a marketplace, check repeat transactions and whether both sides can complete a match. For an annual compliance workflow, look at eligible renewals and completion of the core filing. For developer tooling, check repeat use in real projects after the first successful setup. A pre-launch waitlist gives you a recruitment pool.

For a proposed pivot, name which element changes (buyer, job, channel or solution), what you will hold constant and what evidence would make you keep the current direction. Interview a lost account as well as a retained one. A feature request from the best customer may describe a niche worth serving or a costly exception.
